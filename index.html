Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is My AI Receptionist a replacement for my office staff?

It's intended to help with repetitive lead follow-up and administrative workflows, while your team handles roofing advice, estimates, and customer decisions.

Can it text everyone who calls my business?

Automated texting requires appropriate consent, opt-out handling, and compliance with applicable rules. A missed call alone does not necessarily authorize marketing texts.

Will it book appointments automatically?

Appointment coordination is a planned capability. Live calendar booking requires a scheduling integration and availability rules.

Can I see how many jobs were recovered?

The planned dashboard will show lead activity and potential pipeline value. Actual booked jobs and attributable revenue depend on accurate business records and integrations.
